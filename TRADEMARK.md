# Proscenium trademark policy

The code is free software (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The **name "Proscenium"** and
the **arch logo** are not: they are trademarks of Habiby LLC, and they tell a
writer which program they are getting. This policy exists so that a copy
someone else changed cannot be mistaken for the one we ship.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your software works with Proscenium files, or imports and exports
  them.
- Say your project is "built from Proscenium" or "a fork of Proscenium", with a
  plain statement that it is not the official app and is not endorsed by
  Habiby LLC.
- Write about Proscenium, review it, teach it, and use the name to refer to it.
- Redistribute an official, unmodified build under its own name and icon.

## You may not

- Distribute a modified version under the name "Proscenium" or with the arch
  logo — rename it and give it a different icon.
- Publish anything in an app store, a package registry or a download site under
  the Proscenium name or logo, other than an official build.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests Habiby LLC made, endorses or
  supports your version, product or service.
- Register a domain name, company name or social account that could be
  mistaken for an official Proscenium one.

If you are unsure, ask before you ship. Permission is usually easy to give.
