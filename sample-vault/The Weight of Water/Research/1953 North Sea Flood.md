# 1953 North Sea Flood

Reference material for the family's relationship to the marsh and "the water."

The night of 31 January 1953, a storm tide overwhelmed sea defenses around the North Sea. Water came in the dark, through the floors, before anyone decided anything. People woke already inside it.

Useful for the play's central image: water as the thing that arrives without being invited and stays without being asked — same shape as grief, same shape as a brother in the doorway. Mara measures it now for a living; she's been trying to put a number on what once just came in under the door.
