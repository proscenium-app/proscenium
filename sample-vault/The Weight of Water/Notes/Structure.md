# Structure notes

Two acts. The pipe is the clock.

- **Act One** — one night. The dripping won't stop; neither will Jonah's arriving. End on the blackout before either of them says the true thing.
- **Act Two** — three months later, one scene for now. The tap is capped. The quiet Mara wanted turns out to be the thing she can't stand.

Open thread: does Jonah come back in Act Two, or is his absence the point? Leaving it as a single Act Two scene until I know.

## The shape as it stands

| Scene | Where | Clock | Holds |
|-------|-------|:-----:|-------|
| 1.1 | Kitchen | 11pm | The drip starts. Mara alone. |
| 1.2 | Kitchen | 1am | Jonah arrives with the wrong wrench. |
| 2.1 | Kitchen | +3 months | Capped. Boxes. Nobody says it. |

## What each act has to do

1. Establish the sound, then make it unbearable.
2. Put the two of them in one room and give them a job.
3. Refuse the confession — twice.
   - Once out loud, once by changing the subject.
   - The second refusal is the one that costs.

> The house is not a metaphor. It is a house that leaks. Everything else is the audience's business.

## Rules I keep breaking

- Mara measures; she does not explain. If she explains, cut the line.
  - Exception: the mother. She is allowed one paragraph about the mother, ever.
- Jonah never finishes a sentence he started in another room.
- The drip is a `sound cue`, never a line of dialogue.
