# MARA

A hydrologist, early 40s. Measures everything and trusts nothing she can't measure. Came home to bury her mother and stayed to fight the house.

**Wants:** to stop the dripping — the literal pipe, and everything it stands in for.
**Fears:** that quiet is just absence, not peace.
**Voice:** short sentences. Precise nouns. Doesn't finish the sentimental ones.
