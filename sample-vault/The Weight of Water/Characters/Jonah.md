# JONAH

Mara's younger brother, 30s. Arrives uninvited and won't say why. Inherited "the water" — the family's stake in the marsh — and doesn't know if it's a gift or a debt.

**Wants:** to be let back in without having to ask.
**Fears:** that Mara already decided the answer.
**Voice:** deflects with jokes; means the third thing he says, never the first.
